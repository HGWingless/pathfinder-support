Pathfinder: Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026.

Pathfinder does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

What the app keeps

Your level progress and settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else.

What the app doesn't do

There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs. The app makes no network requests of its own.

Purchases

The in-app purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or Apple ID. Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Because no data is collected, none is collected from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: see Support.
